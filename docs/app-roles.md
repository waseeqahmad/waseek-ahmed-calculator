# App Roles

## Bill Payer

A Bill Payer is the friend who picked up the tab, whether it's dinner, fuel on a road trip or a group food order, and now has to get everyone's share back. Everyone pays them back by bank transfer or Easypaisa/JazzCash, and it all happens in a WhatsApp group.

They can put in what the bill came to, who was there and where to send the money, then see how much each person owes and tick people off as they pay.

They must never end up with shares that don't add back up to the bill, and must never lose track of who's paid without saying so first.

## Group Member

A Group Member is one of the friends who was there and owes the Bill Payer their share. They're in the WhatsApp group, and that's all they want to deal with. They just want to know how much to send and where to send it.

They can see that in a message posted in the group. They never need to touch the app.

They must never have to download anything, sign up for anything or click a link just to pay back a friend.

## How their needs differ

The Bill Payer does the work and needs to keep track over several days. The Group Member does nothing but read one message, so it has to contain one clear amount and where to send it. That need is why friends all owe the same rounded-up number (see S-1) and why the message lists no names (see S-4).
