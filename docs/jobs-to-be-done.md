# Jobs To Be Done

None of these jobs mention the app. Each one would still exist with pen and paper, a phone calculator and a lot of scrolling through WhatsApp.

## J-1 (Bill Payer)

When I've paid for the whole group, I want to work out what each person owes quickly and fairly, so I can ask for the money straight away without doing maths at the table.

## J-2 (Bill Payer)

When friends are sending money back over a few hours or days, I want to keep track of who has paid and who hasn't, so I can tell when I've got everything back and who I still need to chase.

## J-3 (Group Member)

When I owe a friend for something we shared, I want to know exactly how much to send and where, so I can pay them back in a minute from my banking app and get on with my day.
