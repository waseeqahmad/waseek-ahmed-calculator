⏺ The UI plan is written and locked in docs/ui-plan.md. I linked it from
  README.md, and added it to the "Read first" list in CLAUDE.md so the build
  session follows it. Nothing is committed yet.

  Decisions:
  - One screen, no steps. The people list is also the payment tracker, so
    reopening the app tomorrow shows exactly where you left off.
  - Order top to bottom: bill total (a price field with PKR in front), then
    people (your row first, then friends as a checklist), then where to send the
    money (optional), then the message for the group. That's the same order
    things happen at the table.
  - Your name is the first row of the list, with a ticked box that can't be
    changed and paid (you) under it. This shows you count as one of the people
    without needing to explain it.
  - The message preview looks like a chat bubble, with Open in WhatsApp as the
    main button and Copy message next to it. The buttons sit above the bubble,
    because the copy-failure message says "select the text below".
  - Locked state: the total and the add/remove controls lock. Under the total
    there's one note, Locked because someone is ticked as paid., with the Change
    total or people button. Both confirmations appear in place of the button
    that triggered them, with focus on Cancel, and Esc also cancels.
  - Empty fields show grey hints, not red errors, so first use isn't full of
    warnings. Red is only for real mistakes.
  - Look: system font, neutral greys, one green accent, 8px corners everywhere,
    and light and dark mode from the device setting. No new dependencies.

  Where I went beyond the spec, all of it allowed by the acceptance criteria:
  - New bill is hidden while the bill is completely empty.
  - The add-friend field keeps focus after you add someone, so you can type the
    next name straight away.
  - The payment details counter only appears from 180 characters.

  One conflict: the design skill bans the — character, but S-1 requires — for
  empty shares. I kept —, because the spec wins.

⏺ You're right to push on this. Here's my honest take before I ask.

  Why I picked one screen: live feedback. While you type the total and add
  people, you see the shares and the message change, with nothing to tap
  through. It also avoids a "where am I?" problem when you reopen the app days
  later.

  Where you're right: the payer uses the app at two very different moments.
  1. At the table (once, about 1 minute): enter the total, add the people, send
     the message. These belong together, because each change updates the shares
     and the message.
  2. Over the next days (many short visits): "Ali just sent money, tick him."
     For that you only need the checklist and what's still owed. The total
     field, payment box and message are clutter, and the page already locks them
     in this phase anyway.

  So the real split is two phases, not a multi-step wizard. Splitting the setup
  itself (total, then people, then message) would add taps at the table and lose
  the live shares.

  A two-view design also makes S-7 cleaner. The tracker simply has no edit
  fields, and Edit bill brings up the "This will clear 2 payment ticks" warning.
  The cost is some rewording of the acceptance criteria. For example, S-3's
  "message updates straight away when I change the total" assumes the message
  and the total are on the same screen.
